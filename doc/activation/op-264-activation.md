## PREAMBLE — what this project is
rmxOS is an **open-source operating system** — a Darwin/Mach userland ported onto stock FreeBSD 15, developed in the open like FreeBSD or illumos. This is a **maintainer's own code-quality check of ONE feature of our service manager (launchd)** — how launchd keeps a managed job running: it starts the job, notices when its process exits, and restarts it according to the job's KeepAlive policy. Ordinary pre-release code-quality engineering on our own source. No target, no adversary, nothing to exploit — the goal is to confirm this one path is well-built for our own users.

## THE ONE FEATURE (this is the whole scope)
launchd's **job supervision** — the KeepAlive / restart-on-exit path in launchd's job-management core (`core.c`): launchd spawns a job's process, reaps it when it exits, decides whether to respawn it per the job's KeepAlive setting, and throttles a job that exits too fast. This is the control-plane lifecycle that already works (it drives the load→start→observe→restart→remove→reload spine and the cold-boot `KeepAlive:true` auto-start). Confirm this one supervise-and-respawn path is correct — nothing wider.

## CONTEXT
A service manager's core job is: keep the services it owns running — restart one that exits when policy says to, leave one alone when policy says not to, and never hot-loop on a job that fails instantly. This is launchd's working heart and what every hosted daemon (notifyd, asld, our own) relies on to stay up, but the supervision code itself was never read for correctness — this focused pass does that. Plain software-engineering framing: child-exit handling, restart-policy correctness, respawn throttling, and job-record lifetime on unload.

## THE QUESTIONS (all about this one path)
1. **Exit detection & reap.** When a job's process exits, launchd reaps it and records the exit status against the right job — is the reap/attribution correct (no missed child, no exit attributed to the wrong job, no double-reap), and is a job whose process exited left in a consistent state before the restart decision?
2. **KeepAlive restart policy.** On exit, does launchd respawn per the job's KeepAlive setting — restart when it should (KeepAlive:true or a satisfied conditional), and NOT restart when it shouldn't (KeepAlive false, or the job was stopped/removed on purpose)? Any KeepAlive shape that could respawn a job that was meant to stay down, or fail to respawn one that should come back?
3. **Respawn throttling.** A job that exits immediately and repeatedly — is there a throttle (the classic minimum respawn interval) so launchd doesn't spin restarting it, and is that throttle state tracked per-job correctly (one bad job throttled without stalling the others)?
4. **Job-record lifetime on unload/remove.** If a job is unloaded or removed while running or while a respawn is pending, does launchd stop it, cancel any pending respawn, and free the job record cleanly — no orphaned job entry, and no respawn of a job that was just removed?
