## WHAT IT MEANS

When rmxOS grows a **Swift toolchain** (build + run Swift on rmxOS, off the FreeBSD host), it will need a
deliberate answer to **what `-lSystem` resolves to** — the runtime-ABI umbrella every Darwin-targeting link
line expects. This li reserves and designs that answer. It is the home for the rmxOS `-lSystem` solution and
the Swift-platform-triple decision that drives it. **Not a preview item** — the preview routes around it
entirely (see EXCLUSION).

## WHY — the libSystem finding (first-hand, 2026-06-29)

A coverage audit of the NextBSD Darwin surface found rmxOS carries every NextBSD component **except**
`libSystem`. Inspecting NextBSD's `lib/libSystem` first-hand: it is a **97-byte GNU ld linker script**, not a
library —

```
INPUT(libmach.so liblaunch.so libosxsupport.so libdispatch.so libBlocksRuntime.so libpthread.so)
```

— i.e. `-lSystem` textually pulls in six `.so`s. **Zero runtime semantics**: no re-export, no versioned
install-name, no special loader treatment. It is link-line sugar. It is also already **stale against our
tree**: 2 of its 6 INPUT names don't resolve in rmxOS (`libBlocksRuntime` → we ship `libblocksruntime`;
`libpthread` → we ship `libthr`).

Contrast macOS `libSystem.B.dylib`: a real **re-export umbrella** over the `libsystem_*` family where
`libsystem_c` *is* libc and `libsystem_kernel` is the syscall-stub layer — the **implicit bottom-most lib**
that dyld special-cases, such that on Darwin there is no freestanding `-lc` (`-lSystem` *is* the C runtime).
Different category: NextBSD's gives the *name*, none of the *semantics*.

## THE DECISION SPACE (the Swift-triple fork)

`-lSystem` only matters through the lens of how rmxOS presents itself to the Swift/clang toolchain:

- **Path A — FreeBSD-triple (swift-corelibs FreeBSD platform).** The driver links `-lc -lthr -ldispatch
  -lBlocksRuntime …` directly and **never looks for `-lSystem`**. libSystem is *irrelevant*. rmxOS's discrete
  libs already match what this path wants; the only gap is **naming/compat aliases** (a `libBlocksRuntime`
  alias over `libblocksruntime`, a `libpthread` alias over `libthr`). Lower-risk, less-faithful, the likely
  *first* Swift bring-up route.
- **Path B — Darwin-triple (present rmxOS as Apple-like).** The Darwin toolchain auto-injects `-lSystem` and
  treats it as libc+runtime. This needs a **real** libSystem (correct install-name, implicit-link + crt-glue
  semantics, dyld special-casing emulated on FreeBSD's rtld) — the **linker-script trick is insufficient**.
  Macros-primitives-outward faithful endgame (link Apple-built Swift/ObjC binaries unmodified), but a much
  larger commitment.

**Recommendation (Arranger, non-binding — Coordinator owns the call at scope-in):** take **Path A** for the
first Swift bring-up (it needs *no* libSystem — two compat aliases over libs we already ship), and reserve a
**real Path-B libSystem umbrella** as the faithful long-arc endgame. Note: the parts of macOS's libSystem
that are genuinely load-bearing (libc unification, implicit-bottom-lib) are things we *deliberately do not
want* short-term — we keep FreeBSD libc discrete — which is itself a reason Path A fits first.

## EXCLUSION (the standing decision this li records — 2026-06-29)

**NextBSD's `libSystem` linker-script is EXCLUDED from rmxOS — intentionally not carried.** rmxOS does not
ship libSystem today (verified: no `libsystem*` in the rmxOS tree), and will not adopt NextBSD's version: it
is semantics-free, stale against our lib names, and would be cargo-cult. The donor copy stays read-only in
`nx/NextBSD/lib/libSystem` for reference. rmxOS's *own* `-lSystem` answer is deferred to this li and built (if
at all) under Path B. This moves "missing libSystem" off the coverage-gap ledger and onto the
**intentionally-excluded** ledger (catalog under li-1008).

## TRULY-GREEN BAR (what "done" means — only meaningful once scoped in)

1. **Swift-triple decision made** (Path A vs B) and documented, with the platform target rmxOS presents to
   the toolchain pinned.
2. **Path A (if taken):** the compat aliases (`libBlocksRuntime`→`libblocksruntime`, `libpthread`→`libthr`,
   any others a real swift-corelibs FreeBSD link surfaces) exist and a trivial Swift program **builds and
   runs** on rmxOS, linking dispatch + Blocks + libc the FreeBSD way — no libSystem involved.
3. **Path B (if/when taken):** a real `libSystem` umbrella exists with faithful re-export + install-name
   semantics; the Darwin-triple driver's implicit `-lSystem` resolves; an Apple-built (or Darwin-triple)
   Swift binary links and runs. This is the faithful endgame, gated separately.
4. **No regression to the preview floor** — the four core services + substrate are untouched by toolchain work.
