## What milestone 9 MEANS

The **long arc** beyond the 1.0-preview (li-1000) and post-preview hardening (milestone 2, reserved): the
infrastructure and platform-maturity work that makes rmxOS a *development platform*, not just a running set
of core services. Named charter items (from li-1000's reserved-M9 note): the **reproducible release-image
pipeline** (old li-006 → li-9xxx), **self-host** (buildworld-on-rmxOS), **GPU**, and — newly — the
**language/toolchain platform** (Swift toolchain integration + the runtime ABI surface it needs, li-9001).

**Boundary:** nothing here gates the 1.0-preview. A milestone-9 item is, by construction, deferred work; it
is cataloged (li-1008) when it represents a *known gap* the preview deliberately steps around, and authored
in full only when the Coordinator scopes it in.
