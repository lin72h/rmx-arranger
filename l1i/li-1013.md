## WHAT IT MEANS

The **leftover register** for milestone 1: the residual work and open decisions that must be *resolved* or *explicitly Coordinator-dispositioned* before the 1.0-preview can be stamped ship-ready. It is the honest "what's actually left" list, kept separate from its neighbors so nothing hides:

- **NOT li-1008** (*known gaps cataloged*) — those are gaps the preview deliberately **steps around** with a non-blocking justification. A li-1013 item is the opposite: something the preview **cannot just step around** — it either gets done or gets a recorded Coordinator call that it's acceptable.
- **NOT li-1011** (*release-scoping principle*) — that's the *rule* for classifying scope; this is the *worklist* the rule produces.
- **NOT li-9xxx** (*long-arc*) — those are post-preview by construction; a li-1013 item is in-milestone.

Each item records: the finding (verified), the must-do work, the Coordinator decision points, and any contingent ops. An item retires when its work is green (first-hand, overclaim-strict) or its decision is recorded. Nothing here is a *gate* until the Coordinator says so — the list flags, it does not self-decide.
