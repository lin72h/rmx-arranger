# li-1011 — 1.0-preview release-scoping principle (solidify-existing + low-risk macOS-fidelity; risk-exclude deep work)

- tier: **L1i** instruction under milestone **li-1000** (1.0-preview). A *governing* instruction: it does not
  build a feature, it sets the SCOPING RULE every other li-100x / id-NNN / op-NNN is measured against.
- raised: 2026-06-27 (Coordinator directive, codified by the Arranger seat).
- owner: Coordinator owns scope-in/scope-out decisions; Arranger classifies + proposes.

## The principle

The 1.0-preview does **NOT** need feature-parity with its macOS counterpart. It needs the features NextBSD
**already has** to be *solid*, plus the *cheap* fidelity wins taken. Every preview work item sorts into ONE
of three buckets:

1. **Solidify-Existing** — make what NextBSD already implements rock-solid: truly-green, overclaim-strict,
   holds under soak. This is the CORE of the preview. Not new features — durability + correctness on the
   existing surface. (e.g. the four core services {launchd, notify, asl, libxpc} to truly-green.)

2. **Low-Risk macOS-Fidelity** — pick the low-hanging fruit that brings our impl closer to the macOS
   counterpart **without much risk** (small blast radius, no substrate rewrite, observable win).
   **Canonical example: use plist, NOT JSON** for launchd/launchctl config interchange — cheap, faithful,
   low-risk. The litmus: "does this make us more macOS-faithful AND is the change bounded + reversible?"

3. **Risk-Excluded (deferred — NOT in preview)** — deep / high-risk work is explicitly OUT of the preview,
   cataloged as a known gap (li-1008) and deferred to milestone 2+ / the long arc. Named exemplars
   (Coordinator): the **libdispatch firehose**, and **mach-ipc voucher (importance / QoS donation)
   optimization**. These could destabilize the floor; the preview does not gate on them.

**Why:** "preview" is get-it-right > speed (stable + usable, dogfood north star) — but get-it-right here means
*solid on what we have* + *cheap fidelity*, NOT chasing feature-completeness or risky substrate rewrites. A
preview that ships a thin-but-broad surface fails the north star; a preview that ships a narrow-but-solid +
faithful surface succeeds it.

## How to apply (the triage gate)

When authoring or triaging ANY li/id/op for the preview:
- **Classify it** into bucket 1 / 2 / 3.
- Bucket 1 or 2 → **preview-eligible** (bucket 2 still needs the Coordinator's scope-in; it is *cheap*, not
  *free* — it competes for cycles against bucket-1 solidification).
- Bucket 3 → **catalog (li-1008) + defer**. Do not let a bucket-3 item block a truly-green call.
- **Verify before claiming divergence** (feedback_verify_signature_divergence_claims): a bucket-2 "we differ
  from macOS here" must be source/header-confirmed first — several past cited divergences were false. A
  bucket-2 id should be **audit-first** (find + classify the divergence) THEN low-risk-fix the load-bearing
  ones, never a blind "rewrite to match."

  bucket 3 for preview unless the dispatch sub-fix lands cheaply; Coordinator's call.

## Truly-green / retirement

This li is a *standing rule*, retired with li-1000 (the milestone). It "passes" when every preview work item
has a recorded bucket classification and no bucket-3 item is masquerading as a preview gate. Its concrete
spawn is the bucket-2 id stream (id-030 first); bucket-1 is the existing li-100x truly-green ladder.
