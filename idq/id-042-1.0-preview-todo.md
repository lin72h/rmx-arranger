## Final certification bar

### A. Candidate and image identity

- clean, origin-reachable product commit and branch;
- content-addressed image plus guest filesystem/boot configuration proof;
- boot through the intended id-015 UEFI preview path; `bhyveload` or a shell-only component run is
  not equivalent candidate evidence;
- complete core-component manifest with host-build hash → staged-path hash equality;
- ship kernel ident, Mach module flags, libdispatch flags, and per-run dispatch-engine evidence;
- staging-model image is allowed; reproducible buildworld/release-image work is not required.

### B. PID-1 launchd hosting and lifecycle

- `/sbin/launchd` is PID 1 through the accepted `init_path` boot configuration, without `-u`, and
  the accepted `/etc/rc` chain-load brings the FreeBSD base duties up with root read-write;
- real notifyd, asld/ASL maintenance, and a real libxpc/nvlist service consumer are loaded through
  the selected production bridge, not invoked as disconnected shell-only substitutes;
- load→start→observe→restart→remove→reload behavior is exercised where the component contract
  permits it;
- service PIDs, launchd job state, exit status, and restart/coalescing behavior are observed rather
  than inferred from configuration text.

### C. Functional and conservation checks

- notify register/post/check/cancel round-trip;
- ASL native submit/search plus production one-shot reclaim with separate TTL and size controls;
- libxpc object/nvlist round-trip plus Mach send/reply/cancel/error and connection lifecycle;
- dispatch queue/source/timer activity with engine attribution;
- Mach send/receive, port allocation/destruction, enqueue/drain, and dead-name invariants;
- bounded RSS, store size, filesystem capacity, fd **identity**, Mach rights/ports, and child/job
  population. Endpoint counts alone cannot be called leak-free.

### D. Hold and failure bar

- a continuous hours-scale run on the exact candidate. The exact duration is a Coordinator decision
  recorded before fetch; the existing four-hour integration lineage is the planning reference, not
  a new Arranger-authored acceptance ruling;
- zero crash, core, panic, KASSERT/WITNESS/DEADLKRES violation, stuck queue, unbounded resource
  trend, unexpected restart/zombie, restart storm, or lost core-service availability;
- a product failure, harness failure, infrastructure failure, or inconclusive cell is never
  promoted to green;
- complete final inventory, job unload where applicable, buffer sync, and clean guest shutdown.

### E. Evidence and retirement

- raw serial/console and host orchestration records are identity-separated and hashed;
- a Git-derived complete manifest covers every commissioned artifact without self-hash tricks;
- known-bad controls prove artifact mismatch, missing terminal, wrong service/image, and
  pre-path crash are rejected;
- Gatekeeper commits its own evidence by explicit path; no product/control cross-repo write;
- L/XL gates route to Validators. Confidence in the disputed 8–<9 band stops for the Coordinator;
- publication/origin reachability is verified before retirement.
